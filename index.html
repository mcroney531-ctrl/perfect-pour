Hold the button, or hold Space or Enter, to pour. Turn on tap mode to start and stop with single presses. Use the glass switch, or the T key, to tilt the glass and to bring it upright.

Steps in this demo

    Two bands on the training glass: BRING UP (start bringing the glass upright when the beer gets there) and LINE (stop). BRING UP is a training mark that real glasses don't have; on the job, judge it by eye: about half to two-thirds full. Keep holding the pad the whole time: slide up and the glass comes up; stop sliding and it holds its angle while the beer keeps flowing.
